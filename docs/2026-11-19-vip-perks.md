# VIP perks (2026-11-19)

Design: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-19-vip-perks-design.md`. Plan: `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-11-19-vip-perks.md`.
The server half is the outer repo's branch `vip-perks`: `patches/2026-11-19-vip-perks.patch` and a new `Program\7. World.exe`
(`docs/2026-11-19-vip-perks-deploy.md` there).

## What VIP gets now

| | VIP1 | VIP2 | VIP3 | VIP4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Star by the name | bronze | silver | gold | purple |
| Auto Play a week | 24 h | 36 h | 48 h | no limit |
| Gift box (in-game mail) | one per paid month | | | |
| NPC shops | 5% off | 10% off | 15% off | 20% off |
| EXP lost on death | half | half | none | none |

Comfort only: no EXP or drop boost, nothing that makes a VIP stronger.

## What changed in FlyffWeb

**Game (the server decides):**
- The NPC shop window and its tooltips show a VIP the same price the World server charges.
- The purchase tax stays on the full price. Perin, chip shops, selling and personal shops are unchanged.

**The website keeps the in-game VIP star in step with the VIP list:**
- Approving a payment, or the panel's new Give VIP, gives the star.
- A job every 5 minutes (`apps/web/src/vip/vipPerksJob.ts`):
  - takes the star away when VIP ends;
  - puts back any VIP letter a character holds;
  - turns a star set by hand before this update (with no VIP record) into 30 days of VIP;
  - sends the gift boxes.
- Every letter change is in the panel's Log tab, as `vip-job`.
- The game reads the letter when a character enters the world, so a change shows from the player's next login.

**Gift boxes:**
- Each approved request is one gift box, at its tier. Give VIP has a "Send the gift box" tick.
- A box goes by in-game mail, one mail per item stack, to the account's character that is online, or else the one with
  the most play time.
- It waits while the account has no character, or while its tier's box is empty.
- The starting boxes:

  | Item | VIP1 | VIP2 | VIP3 | VIP4 |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Remantis Laccotte | 10 | 20 | 30 | 50 |
  | Refresher Hold | 10 | 20 | 30 | 50 |
  | Vital Drink X | 10 | 20 | 30 | 50 |
  | Town Blinkwing | 5 | 10 | 15 | 20 |
  | Scroll of Resurrection | 1 | 2 | 3 | 5 |

- You change them in the panel: VIP tab, then "Gift box".

**Panel (VIP tab):**
- New views:
  - "Give VIP" (account, level, days, gift tick);
  - "Gift box" (6 item stacks per tier, picked with the Send tab's item search);
  - "Gifts" (the latest boxes, and the character each went to).
- The account and character level pickers no longer offer VIP 1-4.

**The VIP page:** each tier card lists its perks, and a logged-in VIP sees where their latest box went.

**Database (`apps/web/sql/vip.sql`):**
- New tables `WEB_VIP_GIFT` and `WEB_VIP_GIFT_ITEM`, and `CHARACTER_01_DBF.dbo.WEB_VIP_GIFT_SENT`.
- The letter and gift procedures. The mails are written with the game's own `MAIL_STR 'A1'` into server index 51; the
  Database server's real-time mail (`__GETMAILREALTIME`) hands them to the player.
- `Update-FlyffWeb` runs `Install-Vip.ps1` by itself while the panel is on, so nothing extra is needed on the VPS.
- `admin-login.sql`'s level procedures stop taking VIP letters the next time `2-Setup-Database` runs. Until then, the
  panel itself refuses them.

## On the VPS

The steps come with the update bundle (`Bakbak\FlyFF-server-update-<date>\UPDATE-STEPS.txt`):
1. the new `7. World.exe`;
2. this FlyffWeb update (code only, no convert, no gear sync);
3. `Check-Server`.

## Checks to run (VM first)

1. Approve a VIP1 request for a test account and log in: the star shows, and within 5 minutes the gift mail arrives.
2. In an NPC shop, the price shows 5% off and the same amount is charged. Perin is not discounted.
3. Die to a monster: about half the usual EXP is lost. As VIP3, none is lost.
4. Set the account's VIP end in the past (panel: Give VIP cannot shorten it; in SQL,
   `UPDATE ACCOUNT_DBF.dbo.WEB_VIP SET endsAt = '2020-01-01' WHERE account = 'tester1'`). Within 5 minutes and after a
   new login: no star, full prices, normal death loss.
5. A GM account keeps its GM letter, VIP or not.
6. Give VIP for 3 days with the gift tick off: the star shows and no gift is sent.
