# Piece 7 M2 — Job Change and the First-Job Weapons Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** After the server's job change, the Mercenary, Acrobat, Assist and Magician play as in Neuz V15. The windows
follow the change. Every first-job weapon attacks as Neuz has it: the wand's charged missiles, the bow's arrows, the
yoyo's reach. Others' missiles and arrows are seen. The first-job skills work: the magic casts, the skill missiles, the
heals and buffs on a friendly target. Every packet is byte for byte Neuz's.

**Architecture:** In two parts, built in order on one branch.
- **Part A, the job change and the weapons (Tasks 1-10).** The protocol learns the job change, the ranged attacks and the
  hit reports (Task 1). The catalog and the converter add what the new code reads (Task 2). The job change goes through
  the states and windows (Tasks 3-4). The action mover learns the wand's and bow's attacks (Task 5). A DOM-free
  `Missiles` module flies every missile and sends the hit reports through `OwnShots` (Task 6). The effect player draws
  missiles and mesh parts (Task 7). The attack loop gains the charge, the shots and the yoyo (Tasks 8-9). Task 10 wires
  them into the world and adds `jobs.spec.ts`.
- **Part B, the first-job skills (Tasks 11-16).** Magic casting for every player (Task 11). The skill missiles (Task 12).
  The skill effects and the from-behind rule (Task 13). Buffs: their messages and their numbers (Task 14), their icons and
  the wiring of Part B (Task 15). The checklist and the checkpoint (Task 16).

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript, Vite, Vitest (jsdom for DOM tests), Playwright (Chromium), Three.js, the FlyffWeb converter.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-03-piece7-design.md` §3.3 (M2), §4, §5 (M2 row), §7 (M2). Research (cited as
`[R §n]`): `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-03-piece7-m2-jobs-research.md`. C++ paths are relative to `Blouflash VS22
V15/Source/`.

## Global Constraints

- No server or gateway change: every packet is one Neuz sends, with Neuz's bytes [R §5].
- Each new module opens with the C++ it copies as a `Function  File:lines  what` table, as `hud/logOutBox.ts:1-23` does.
- Server and catalog text goes into the page with `textContent`, never as markup.
- Strings come from the texts catalog (`data.texts`, keyed by TID name), with the English of [R] as the fallback.
- The phone rule is `(max-width: 1000px) and (pointer: coarse)`; phone window text stays at least 14 px.
- Custom server features (`CUSTOM_FEATURES.md`) are out of scope. The job change chain (the quests) is M1's.
- No new dependency.
- Builders run `npx vitest run <the task's test files>`, `npm run typecheck`, and at most the one browser spec their task
  changes, through the port-5175 config (Setup). The owner's `dev:client` holds 5174: never stop it.
- **M1 runs at the same time** and touches `WorldScreen.ts`, `OwnState.ts`, `packages/protocol/src/world/readers.ts`,
  `style.css` and `docs/device-testing.md`. M2's logic goes in its own modules. In those five files M2 adds lines and
  changes as few existing ones as it can, so that the rebase onto M1 stays small.

## Review Focus

1. **A shot whose target dies or leaves view in flight.** The missile goes and sends SFX_CLEAR, never SFX_HIT, and nothing
   throws. Test: Task 6 Step 1, "a target gone or dead in flight clears the shot".
2. **A shot spammed on a phone.** Rapid taps of the Attack button with a wand send at most one MAGIC_ATTACK per motion and
   never one during the 1800 ms lock-out after an automatic shot. Test: Task 8 Step 1, "no shot during the lock-out, and none
   while the last is still in the air of its motion".
3. **The bow's last arrow.** UPDATE_ITEM takes the arrow stack to 0: the next press says "Equip arrows first." and sends
   nothing. Test: Task 8 Step 1, "the last arrow spent, the next shot is refused".
4. **A world change, a death or a log-out with missiles in the air.** The missiles go with the world, without reports sent
   into the new world, and no effect stays on the screen. Test: Task 6 Step 1, "dispose drops every missile without
   reporting"; Task 10 Step 3, where `disposeWorld` calls it.
5. **Another player or a monster shooting while the own player is in a dialogue or shop.** The readers keep the bundle
   whole, the missile flies, and the windows stay as they are. Test: Task 1 Step 1, "a bundle with MAGIC_ATTACK,
   RANGE_ATTACK and CREATESFXALLOW is read to its end".

```ts
/** m_idSfxHit and the reports of the own player's missiles (CSfx::DamageToTarget, ~CSfx; SendSfxID/Hit/Clear). */
export class OwnShots {
  constructor(session: Pick<WorldSession, 'sendSfxId' | 'sendSfxHit' | 'sendSfxClear'>);
  /** m_idSfxHit as it is: what MAGIC_ATTACK and RANGE_ATTACK carry. */
  get current(): number;
  /** ++m_idSfxHit, then SFX_ID: the new id. */
  register(targetId: number, type: number, skill?: number, maxDmgCnt?: number): number;
  hit(sfxHitId: number, power: number, skill: number, attackerId: number): void;
  clear(sfxHitId: number, moverId: number): void;
}
export type MissileClass = 'wand' | 'arrow';                        // Part B adds the skills' classes
export interface MissileLaunch {
  cls: MissileClass;
  sourceId: number; targetId: number;
  from: Vec3;
  flightXi: number; hitXi: number;                                 // -1: none
  scale: number;                                                   // the wand's charge scale
  /** Who reports the hit: the own player (own), the own player for a monster's missile at it (forMonster), nobody (null). */
  report: { sfxHitId: number; power: number; skill: number; attackerId: number } | null;
}
export interface MissileView { id: number; xi: number; pos: Vec3; rotation: Vec3; scale: number; frame: number }
export interface MissileWorld {
  movers: ReadonlyMap<number, { act: { body: { pos: Vec3 }; isDie(): boolean } }>;
  /** The target's box (m_vMin/m_vMax) and x scale: the wand's aim point and radius. */
  boxOf(moverId: number): { min: Vec3; max: Vec3; scale: number } | undefined;
}
export class Missiles {
  constructor(shots: Pick<OwnShots, 'hit' | 'clear'>);
  launch(launch: MissileLaunch): number;
  /** One 60 Hz tick: moves, arrives, reports. Returns the hit effects to play this tick. */
  step(world: MissileWorld): Array<{ xi: number; pos: Vec3; angle: number; sourceId: number; targetId: number }>;
  /** What to draw: one view per missile in flight. */
  views(): readonly MissileView[];
  /** Drops every missile and reports nothing (a world change, a log-out). */
  dispose(): void;
}
```

## Decisions this plan makes

1. **No review stop.** The owner said on 2026-11-03 to go on with the plan while away. It is built by the Native route,
   as M0 was, with one fresh review of the whole branch at the end.
2. **Two parts, one checkpoint.** Part A (the change and the weapons) and Part B (the skills and buffs) are built in
   order; the owner checks both at the end.
3. **The charge "effect" is Neuz's gauge**, a 2D bar over the head, not an `XI_*` effect [R §3]. The charge also shows in
   the wand missile's size.
4. **Phones:** with a wand or a bow, the Attack button is a press on the focused target while held, and its let-go when
   lifted: held it charges, lifted it shoots. Other weapons keep today's button. A press held on the monster works as on
   the PC.
5. **Another player's wand shot blocked by the line of sight** is dropped without the message Neuz would print for it.
6. **Not drawn in M2:** the arrows' trails (`etc_Tail2`: the arrow, Junk Bow's three), the wand missile in flight
   (`XI_FIR_WAND02`, M5), the hand-linked skill effects (`MoverSkill.cpp:905-988`).
7. **Ranged monsters' hits on the own player** are reported as Neuz reports them (SFX_HIT with the monster as the
   attacker). Browser players now take ranged monsters' damage, which they did not before.
8. **Blinkpool** (level 30, a point on the ground) is refused with a message: the browser has no cursor point for it yet.
9. **The own buffs at login** are not read: they come after fields of the own ADD_OBJ the browser does not read yet
   (banks, pet, titles). They show from the next cast. Other movers' ADD_OBJ buffs are kept.
10. **No keyboard auto-attack shortcut** (`MOT_BASE_AUTOATTACK`): the browser has no motion shortcuts.
11. **Hot Air** sends the one SFX_HIT Neuz sends; its later hits are the server's.
12. **The first step of `CSfxShoot` and the wave from the world's origin** (a 0.2-0.25 m skew) is not copied.
13. **Water Ball's leftover shots** are cleared when its motion ends; Neuz carries them into the next skill.
14. **The job change chain** (the quests) is M1's. M2's checks use GM-made characters.
15. **Phone buff icons** stay at least 28 px and sit under the status window.
