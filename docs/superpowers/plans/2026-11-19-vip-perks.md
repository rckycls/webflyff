# VIP Perks Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Give VIP1-4 a gift box by in-game mail for each paid month, 5/10/15/20% off NPC shops and less EXP lost on
death, with the website keeping the in-game VIP letter in step with the payments list.

**Architecture:**
- The World server gets one patch (shop price and death EXP), built into a new `7. World.exe`.
- FlyffWeb gets:
  - new SQL in `apps/web/sql/vip.sql` (ACCOUNT_DBF and CHARACTER_01_DBF);
  - a 5-minute job in the website, which fixes letters and sends gifts;
  - panel controls (Give VIP, gift contents, gift log);
  - the VIP price in the client's shop window;
  - the perks on the VIP page.
- One shared table of numbers lives in `packages/protocol`, and a C++ header mirrors it with the same examples.

**Tech Stack:**
- C++ (VS 2022, `all.sln -t:WorldServer`, Release|Win32).
- SQL Server 2022 T-SQL.
- TypeScript (Node site in `apps/web`, browser client in `apps/client`, vitest).

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-19-vip-perks-design.md` (FlyffWeb). The owner approved it on 2026-11-19.

## Global Constraints

**What VIP gets**
- Comfort only: no EXP, drop or stat bonus anywhere.
- VIP level ↔ letter: 1↔`A`, 2↔`B`, 3↔`C`, 4↔`E`. `F` is a normal player. Any other letter is never changed.
- In SQL, compare letters as binary (`CAST(x AS binary(1)) IN (0x46, 0x41, 0x42, 0x43, 0x45)`). The collation is case-insensitive, and lower case letters are admin.

**Shop discount**
- 5, 10, 15 or 20 whole percent off: `price = cost - floor(cost * pct / 100)`, in integers.
- Never discounted: Perin (item 26456, `II_SYS_SYS_SCR_PERIN`), and any item whose discounted price is ≤ its NPC buy-back
  for one unit (`max(1, GetCost() / 4)`).
- The purchase tax is worked out on the price before the discount.
- Only NPC shop buying is discounted. Repairs, selling, chip shops and personal shops stay as they are.

**Death EXP**
- The EXP lost on death is multiplied by 0.5 for VIP1-2 and 0 for VIP3-4.
- Chaotic players always get 1.

**Gifts**
- One gift per approved request, and one per Give VIP when its gift tick is on.
- Each gift is sent within 5 minutes to the account's character on server `01` that is online now, or else the one with
  the most `TotalPlayTime`. Ties go to the lowest `m_idPlayer`. Deleted characters (`isblock = 'D'`) never count.
- One mail per item stack, through `dbo.MAIL_STR 'A1'` into server index `51`, the way `MAIL_STR_REALTIME 'I1'` does it.
- Title `VIP <n> gift` and text `Thank you for supporting osflyff.online!`. Sender `0000000`, no penya.
- At most 6 item stacks per tier.

**Letters and the job**
- A VIP letter on an account or character with no `WEB_VIP` row at all becomes a 30-day VIP at that level, with no gift.
- The job runs every 5 minutes, first 60 s after start, only while both the `vip` and `admin` sections are on.

**SQL rules**
- The `vipSql.test.ts` rules stay: `usp_Admin*` procedures go only to `flyffweb_admin` and `usp_Web*` only to
  `flyffweb_site`, typed parameters, no SQL built from text, no table grants.

**Server patch**
- `patches/2026-11-19-vip-perks.patch` goes after the 13 applied patches, ending with `2026-11-18-rates-x77`.

**Working rules**
- Work only in the two new worktrees.
- Never touch the owner's main folders, merge, push, or apply the patch to main `Source`.
- No secrets: FlyffWeb (webflyff) is a public repo.
- Quick tests only: typecheck and the vitest files named here. No e2e, which the owner runs.

## Review Focus

1. A GM or admin account that also has `WEB_VIP` must keep its staff letter after the job and after approval.
   Tested in Task 3 (SQL text) and Task 5 (memory store).
2. An account with no character, or only deleted ones: the gift stays unsent and is retried, never marked sent.
   Tested in Task 5.
3. A crash or a second job run between writing the mails and marking the gift sent must not send it twice.
   `WEB_VIP_GIFT_SENT`'s key is what stops it. Tested in Task 3 (SQL text) and Task 5.
4. Cheap items and Perin: a 20% price at or below the buy-back must charge, and show, the full price.
   Tested in Task 1 and the Task 8 check.
5. VIP ends: the next job run puts `A`/`B`/`C`/`E` back to `F` on the account and its characters. Tested in Task 5.

     waiting.
