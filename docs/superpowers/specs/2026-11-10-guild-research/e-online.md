Caveat: most accessible sources are the gPotato-Brazil FlyFF wiki (last edited Aug 2019, describes the Gala/Universe-era classic client, level cap 120+) and Madrigal Inside (FlyFF Universe/private-server community). None is dated to V15 (2009-2010). Treat all numbers as "classic FlyFF, late era", not proven V15. No YouTube/screenshot URLs could be fetched; nothing found for the exact V15 window.

## 1. Creating a guild (W, M)
- NPC: Helena, Saint Morning, the Bank NPC.
- Level 40 required. 3,000,000 penya. A party of 3 (leader + 2 others), all near the NPC; the 2 others must not have been in any guild in the last 48 hours.
- Flow: talk to Helena, she takes the 3M penya, then asks for a 3-player party, talk again, choose "Create a Guild". The quest part is needed only once per character; later guilds just need the 3-player eligible party at Helena (W, M).
- Naming rules: not found. W says the guild name can be changed once (Info tab "Name" button). Character limits not stated.

## 2. Guild levels (W table; M unlock list)
- Leveling needs two contributions: quest items ("Trophy") and penya, donated through the guild warehouse's support button (W). Quest item exp is about monster level / 2 (M). Items dropped plainly into the warehouse do not count toward leveling. Donations cannot be retrieved. The guild window shows needed vs donated penya and exp (M).

## 3. Ranks (W, M)
- Names: Master/Leader above Kingpin, Captain, Supporter, Rookie (W lists tier order Rookie < Supporter < Captain < Kingpin; M: Master/Leader, Kingpins, Captains, Supporters, Rookies). Counts per rank: not found.
- Member tab (W): Promote/Demote between ranks (only for members below your own rank and class); Class Up/Class Down changes 1 to 3 icons beside the name within a rank; Create Nickname (leader only, guild level 10); Leave Guild (own leave, and leader uses it to kick).
- Title tab (W): a grid, one column per rank, checkboxes per permission: Title (change classification of lower members), Rank (change class of lower members), Join (invite unguilded players), Penya (withdraw penya from warehouse), Items (withdraw items), Pay (salary for the rank group, paid every 24 hours). M says titles are picked from a dropdown with checkboxes for powers.
- G (one guild's own rules, not game defaults): mentions warehouse access for Supporters.

## 4. Invite/join/leave/kick/disband
- Invite needs the Join permission (W). 48-hour no-guild window applies when forming a guild; whether it applies to joining is not stated.
- Leave and kick via "Leave Guild" in member menu (W). Dismiss button on the Info tab disbands (W). No other cooldown or penalty found.
- M: Guild Master can set listing in guild search, open join or approval join, plus a recruiting notice (likely Universe addition).

## 5. Notice, logo, cloak (W, M)
- Notice: editable any time on Info tab; short character limit (no number).
- Logo: chosen once, cannot be changed (W); M says a Scroll of G-Logo Change removes it, then the master picks again. Cost not found.
- Cloak: leader presses Cloak button, cloaks are generated into the Guild Warehouse once a logo is chosen. Not tradeable, so a member needs the Items-withdraw permission to take their own. Cost not found. Shows the guild logo (M).

## 7. War modes
- Guild war (declare war): W Guild War tab: "Guild War" button then type the target guild name; "Peace" sends a truce request to the other leader; "Give Up" ends it as a loss. The tab shows total wins/losses. Duel rules (P): leader holds Alt and clicks the enemy leader, chooses Guild Duel, other leader accepts; ends when a leader is killed, surrenders or truce; max 2 hours; if leaders log out, the one online longest wins; members need no party. No penya cost found. M says 1vs1 Guild Duel needs guild level 6, peace level 14.
- Guild Siege (S): guild level 20, leader applies at GuildWar Manager Frankie, North-Flarine; bid minimum 100,000,000 penya (200,000,000 after a win), max 2,147,483,647; 3 to 8 guilds; bidding closes 1 hour before; lineup 2 to 10 members (first 5 warped in); one defender; 10 lives each, free-for-all; points: kill 2, leader kill +1, defender kill +1, last-life kill +1, each remaining life +1 to guild; winner gets Champion boxes (Dark Devil's cloaks), Red Chips, buff. W says siege Saturday 7:30 PM UTC. DISAGREEMENT: MS (Universe) says top-10 guilds, 15 participants, 40-minute combat; a gitbook says 6 lives. Siege numbers differ by server/version, all post-V15 likely.
- Guild Battle Royal (W): GM-run PvM event for 16 members, cancelled.
- Guild house: T confirms V15 (April 2010 article) added "a new Guild House system", no detail. W: "guilds can now have a club house", no detail. H (FlyFF Universe, not V15): guild ships over Madrigal, bid for with guild bank, 2-week cycle, artifact defend/destroy. Probably not what V15 did.
- Guild bank / warehouse (W): at all Bank NPCs (Server 1 note); anyone can deposit items by drag and drop; withdraw needs Items permission; penya via support button, withdraw needs Penya permission; works like the personal bank.
