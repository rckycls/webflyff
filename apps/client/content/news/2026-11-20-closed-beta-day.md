# Closed beta day: bosses, Redeem Codes and more

The closed beta opened on November 20 at 4 PM. Thank you for joining!

- **Bosses:** big bosses and giants have layered HP bars, and bosses come back twice as fast.
- **Drops:** penya and item drops are now x10. EXP stays x77.
- **Redeem Code** (Start → Extras): type a code from our Discord or Facebook to get items or VIP.
- **Loading screen** with a progress bar when you enter the world or change maps.
- **Mouse:** the wheel zooms, and the camera and clicks work like in the classic game.
- **Discord:** a button on the home page takes you to our community.
- **Fix:** items received in a trade after using the bag's Sort button now land in the right place.

Found a bug? Report it in **#bug-reports** on our [Discord](https://discord.gg/wJTREXRPSE). Bug reports that get fixed are rewarded.
