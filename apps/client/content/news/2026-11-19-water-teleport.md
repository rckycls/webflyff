# Water, Teleport, Awaken, Card crafting and VIP perks

- **Water:** lakes and seas now show, and so does the sea of clouds below the island edges.
- **Teleport** (Start → Extras): go to Flaris, Saint Morning or Darkon for 10,000 penya. VIP members can reach 11 more places.
- **Awaken** (Start → Extras): awaken your gear with a Scroll of Awakening for 50,000 penya. Add a Scroll of Reversion to replace an old awakening.
- **Craft Card** at Bozman in Saint Morning: put a Craft Stone and up to five cards together for a chance at a 7% card (or an A weapon card). Better cards give better chances; if it fails, the stone and the cards are used up. Craft Stones drop from monsters everywhere.
- **VIP perks:** discounts at NPC shops (5% to 20%), less or no EXP lost when you die, Sort and penya-to-Perin buttons in the bag, a gift box for each paid month, more Teleport places, more Auto Play time and Card crafting in bulk.
