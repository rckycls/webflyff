# Backpack pockets, View Equipment and job change gear

- **Backpack:** extra bag pockets, on PC and phone.
- **View Equipment:** look at another player's gear.
- **Options:** the full Game and Audio settings.
- **Shout balloons** over players' heads.
- **Job change gear:** finishing your 1st job (level 15) or 2nd job (level 60) through the job quests now gives a set of rare gear.
- **Safer characters:** deleting a character now asks for your bank PIN.
