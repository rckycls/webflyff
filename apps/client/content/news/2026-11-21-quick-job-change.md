# Quick Job Change and more control options

- **Quick Job Change:** when you reach your next job's level, a window lets you pick your new job. You get the same rewards as the job quests. It works for the 1st and 2nd job, Master and Hero, and you can also open it from Start → Extras → Job Change.
- **Teleport:** the wait is now 2 seconds, and the window closes as you move.
- **Phones:** dragging a finger up now tilts the view up, like most mobile games. If you prefer the old way, turn on **Invert Camera** in Options.
- **PC:** the ten shortcut icons are now on the PC screen too. The Auto Play icon opens the Auto Play window, and Start and Stop are inside it.
