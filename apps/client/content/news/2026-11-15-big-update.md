# Big update: housing, guild bank, Guild Siege and more

- **Housing:** your own room, and guild houses with furniture you can place.
- **Guild bank** for every guild.
- **Guild Siege**, with the Siege Ranking window.
- **Social:** Cheers, Couple, Follow and Duel.
- **Beauty shop:** change your hair and face (coupons work too).
- **Red Chip shop** at Waforu.
- **Auto Play** (Start menu): farming, potions and skills on their own, with a Support tab for buffers.
- **Better item tooltips:** cooldowns, time left, and a side-by-side comparison with what you wear.
