FlyFF in your browser: the classic game, played on a PC, a tablet or a phone, with nothing to install.

**Rates:** EXP x77, penya and item drops x10.

- **The classic world:** every job up to Master and Hero, guilds, Guild Siege, housing, pets, personal shops and trade.
- **Made for phones too:** touch controls, the game's own keyboard, and windows that fit a small screen.
- **Extras** in the Start menu: Auto Play, Pet Filter, Teleport, Awaken, Quick Job Change and Redeem Code. Card crafting is at Bozman in Saint Morning.

This server is in **closed beta**. Things may change, and progress may be reset before the full launch.

Found a bug? Tell us in **#bug-reports** on our [Discord](https://discord.gg/wJTREXRPSE).
